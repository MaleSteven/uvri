UGANDA VIRUS RESEARCH INSTITUTE

Studying the viruses that shape public health in East Africa.

The Uganda Virus Research Institute is a country’s national reference
laboratory for viral disease, supporting the Ministry of Health with
surveillance, diagnostics, and research into arboviral diseases,
viral haemorrhagic fevers, HIV, and emerging pathogens.
