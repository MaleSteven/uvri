UGANDA VIRUS RESEARCH INSTITUTE

Studying the viruses that shape public health in East Africa

The Uganda Virus Research Institute is a country's national reference laboratory for viral disease,
supporting the Ministry of Health in surveillance, diagnostics, and research into arboviral diseases,
haemorrhagic fevers, HIV, and emerging pathogens.
