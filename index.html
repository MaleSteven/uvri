UGANDA VIRUS RESEARCH INSTITUTE

Protecting Uganda, One Discovery at a Time.

Welcome to UVRI. We are more than just a laboratory on Nakiwogo Road in Entebbe — we are your neighbours, your family, and a team of Ugandan scientists dedicated to keeping our communities safe.

Every day, our team wakes up with one purpose: to study the viruses that affect our people. From HIV, Ebola, and Marburg to the new threats that appear without warning, we track them, we test them, and we find ways to stop them.

We work hand-in-hand with the Ministry of Health and health workers across the country because we believe science is only useful when it serves the people. This is our home, this is our calling, and we are proud to serve Uganda.
