UGANDA VIRUS RESEARCH INSTITUTE

Studying the viruses that shape public health in East Africa.

The Uganda Virus Research Institute is a country’s national reference laboratory for viral disease, supporting the Ministry of Health with surveillance, diagnostics, and research into arboviral diseases, viral haemorrhagic fevers, HIV, and emerging pathogens.

Key Focus Areas

  • Arbovirology & Viral Hemorrhagic Fevers
  • Immunology & HIV Reference Testing
  • Epidemiological Surveillance & Diagnostics
  • Emerging Pathogen Outbreak Response

The Uganda Virus Research Institute is a medical research institute owned by the Uganda government that carries out research on communicable diseases in man and animals, with emphasis on viral transmitted infections.

Key Details

Establishment: Founded in 1936 to investigate a yellow fever outbreak, the institute celebrated its 90th anniversary in 2026.

Location: Entebbe, Uganda (with field stations including the Zika Forest).

Administration: Funded and administered through the Uganda National Health Research Organization under the Uganda Ministry of Health.

Reference Laboratories

Operates as a national and regional reference center for vector-borne and infectious viral diseases, WHO influenza laboratory, yellow fever reference lab, and HIV quality assurance lab
