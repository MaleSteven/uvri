Project Brief

Vision To be a world-class center of excellence in viral and health research

MissionTo conduct scientific investigations on viral diseases, contribute to knowledge, innovation, policy, practice, and capacity strengthening.

GoalTo contribute to the reduction of the viral disease burden for improved public health and security.

Core valuesProfessionalism, Ethics, Effectiveness, and Teamwork: Maintaining high standards of competence, integrity, quality, and collaboration across all research and service delivery.

Core Objectives

Scientific Investigation: Conduct robust research on viral and other communicable diseases.

Knowledge &Innovation: Contribute to scientific knowledge, innovations, and evidence-based health policy and practice.

Capacity Strengthening: Build and maintain a skilled workforce and infrastructure for effective research and service delivery.
