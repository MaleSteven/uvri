About Us

Studying the viruses that shape public health in East Africa.

The Uganda Virus Research Institute is a country’s national reference laboratory for viral disease, supporting the Ministry of Health with surveillance, diagnostics, and research into arboviral diseases, viral haemorrhagic fevers, HIV, and emerging pathogens.

Institute Record

  • Founded: 1937
  • Location: Entebbe, Uganda
  • Oversight: Ministry of Health
  • Designation: WHO Collaborating Centre

Vision

To be a world-class center of excellence in viral and health research.

Mission & Core Objectives

  • Scientific Investigation: Conduct robust research on viral and other communicable diseases.
  • Knowledge & Innovation: Contribute to scientific knowledge, innovations, and evidence-based health policy and practice.
  • Capacity Strengthening: Build institutional sustainability, train scientists, and develop regional public health capacity.

Core Values

  • Research Ethics: Operating strictly in line with established scientific and institutional research ethics.
  • Public Interests: Prioritizing community welfare and health needs.
  • Collective Efforts: Rooting long-term success in mutual trust, teamwork, and shared institutional goals.
